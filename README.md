# Goal Mountain

A small, free tool for keeping a habit going while you work toward one big goal. Plan your week, tick things off, watch your skills level up, and climb a mountain one step at a time, with rewards along the way.

**Try it:** https://rokaveni.github.io/goal-mountain/

## Before you start: please read

**This is a motivation and progress tracker, not a learning app.**
It doesn't teach you anything and has no lessons or exercises. It's not Duolingo. It helps you show up consistently and see your effort adding up, whatever you're working on. I used it for Finnish, and I'll share what I actually studied in each category separately, but the tool itself works for any goal.

**I'm not a developer.**
My original system is fully manual: I colour in progress bars by hand and keep track of my time myself. After my post about passing the YKI got more attention than I expected, people asked how I did it, so I used Claude (an AI assistant) to turn that system into something interactive that anyone can use. That means it has limitations, and I don't plan to keep developing it beyond small tweaks.

**Your data stays in your browser, and only there.**
- Nothing you type is sent anywhere. There are no accounts and no tracking, and there's no AI running inside the app. I can't see your data, and neither can anyone else.
- The flip side: your progress only exists in the browser you use. If you open the page on another device, in another browser or in a private window, you'll start from zero. Clearing your browser data erases it.
- To be safe, download a backup file now and then from *Settings → Backup*. Loading that file on another device moves your progress there.

## How it works

1. **Pick your pace** in *Settings*: how many hours a week you can give your goal, on whichever days suit you (weekdays, evenings, only weekends). The mountain is sized to match, so it feels about the same whether you have 2.5 or 20 hours a week. Your pace then locks, so you don't accidentally reshuffle your progress.
2. **Name your goal and add your categories**: the areas you want to work on (for a language, maybe listening, speaking, reading, writing and grammar).
3. **Plan your week** on the *Climb* tab: pick a day and add what you want to do, like "Grammar, 30 min, workbook chapter". The plan repeats every week, and a line under it shows roughly how fast you'll climb at that pace.
4. **Tick things off** as you do them. The minutes go straight into that category's ring.
5. **Level up.** When a ring fills, that category levels up and your hiker climbs one step.
6. **Reach checkpoints.** Every 10 steps (about once a month at your planned pace) you hit a checkpoint and get a reward. Set your rewards in advance on the *Rewards* tab.
7. **Log extra time** in *Log* when you did something that wasn't on the plan.
8. **Reach the summit.** You get a choice: extend this mountain with more steps and keep climbing with your current levels, or start a brand-new mountain for your next goal (you can keep your categories and weekly plan). Finished goals are kept in a *Summits reached* list in *Settings*.

The app opens with example data so you can see how it looks. Today's list includes demo sessions you can tick to see a level-up, a checkpoint and the summit. Press **Start fresh** to begin with your own goal.

## Good to know

- On your phone, use your browser's *Add to Home Screen* option to save it like an app.
- Each category levels up on its own, so the more categories you have, the slower it can feel at the start.
- Changing your pace mid-climb recalculates every level from your logged time, so your hiker may move up or down. That's why the pace settings are locked until you choose to unlock them.
- Removing a category also removes the time logged for it.
